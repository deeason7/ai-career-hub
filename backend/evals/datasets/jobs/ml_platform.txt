Machine Learning Engineer, NLP Platform

Join the team that puts language models into our product surface. This role is as much about
measurement as modeling: we do not ship a model without an evaluation set behind it.

What you will do:
- Fine-tune and evaluate transformer models for classification and extraction tasks
- Build and maintain labeled evaluation sets, and report agreement between annotators
- Own the retraining pipeline in Airflow, including drift monitoring on inputs
- Optimize inference for CPU serving, including quantization
- Work in Python with PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers

What we are looking for:
- 3+ years applying NLP or machine learning in production
- Fluency with PyTorch, scikit-learn, and the Hugging Face ecosystem
- MLOps instincts: reproducible training, versioned data, monitored deployments
- SQL and comfort with Docker and AWS
