Analytics Engineer

Our data team is looking for an analytics engineer to own the transformation layer between
raw operational data and the models the business makes decisions on.

What you will do:
- Own a dbt project: models, tests, documentation, and lineage
- Write the SQL that turns logistics and retail events into trustworthy marts
- Schedule and monitor transformations in Airflow, with alerting on data freshness
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Looker for operations leadership
- Define metrics once, in one place, and get teams to agree on them

What we are looking for:
- 4+ years of advanced SQL and dimensional data modeling
- Production dbt experience, including testing strategy
- Python and pandas for the work SQL should not do
- Snowflake or a comparable cloud warehouse
