Warehouse Associate, Night Shift

Our distribution center is hiring associates for the overnight sorting line. This is a
physical role on the floor, and no prior warehouse experience is required.

What you will do:
- Load, unload, and sort packages onto the correct outbound lanes
- Operate a pallet jack and, after certification, a sit-down forklift
- Scan inventory into the handheld terminal and flag damaged freight
- Keep the aisles and staging areas clear and to safety standard
- Meet hourly throughput targets set by the shift supervisor

What we are looking for:
- Able to lift 50 pounds repeatedly across an eight-hour shift
- Reliable attendance for overnight hours, Sunday through Thursday
- Attention to detail when scanning and labeling
- Forklift certification is a plus but we will train the right candidate
